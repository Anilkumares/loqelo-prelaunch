    About Loqelo

    One place for everything your home, garden and office needs.

    Loqelo connects people who need a job done with service providers who can do it. You describe the job once, in your own words, and providers who offer that service in your area can send you an offer.

    For service providers, Loqelo is a simple way to find requests that match their skills and working area, and to reply with an offer in a few steps.

    How it works

    From one sentence to the right provider.

    Three steps, from describing what you need to choosing who does it.

    Join the waitlist
    1. 01

      Step 1: Describe your need

      Tell Loqelo what your home, garden or office needs, in your own words. Add photos if they help. Loqelo recognises the services in your request.

    2. 02

      Step 2: Get matched

      Your request reaches service providers who offer those services in your area, so the right people can see it and respond.

    3. 03

      Step 3: Compare offers and choose

      Offers arrive with price, timing and reviews side by side. Ask questions, compare, then pick the provider that suits you.
